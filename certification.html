💩 Sheeternetes Fundamentals exam

The foundational examination in the CSFE program. Pass with 7/10 and a verifiable credential is issued to your GitHub identity — a sequential serial in the public registry, valid until the heat death of the universe. Already certified? Verify or download your certificate →

Examination policy

• Format: 10 multiple-choice items. Passing score: 7/10 (70%).

• Duration: unlimited. Time has no meaning in the spreadsheet.

• Attempts: unlimited, non-proctored, open-book, open-heart.

• Prerequisites: none. Ambition is sufficient.

You passed. Claim your credential.

Issued to the GitHub account you file the request with — you certify yourself. A GitHub Action assigns a sequential serial (SFE000001, …), records you in the public registry, and closes the request with your verification link.

Claim my credential on GitHub →

Requires a GitHub account and one click on “Submit new issue”. Issuance takes seconds.
