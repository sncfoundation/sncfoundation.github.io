We are the only cloud-native foundation with a Total Cost of Ownership of exactly zero and a Return on Investment of minus 340%. Below are our open letters to the companies we would be honored to reconcile with.

The Sheet-Native Computing Foundation is a non-profit, entirely open-source foundation dedicated to a simple thesis: that cloud-native infrastructure does not require a cloud, a cluster, or a budget — only a spreadsheet, and the courage to reconcile it. We were founded on 2 September 2026. In the weeks since, we have shipped more of a future nobody requested than most foundations ship in a decade.

Because we believe in transparency — and because we could not find anyone's private email — we are publishing our sponsorship requests in the open. Read them. Enjoy them. Forward them to someone with a budget.
