Our other projects

The people behind the SNCF also build other things — some serious, some not, most somewhere in between.

Sankofa

A museum of seven centuries of Black culture and long multimedia special projects about it: music, film, photography. Sankofa is also a Kubernetes distribution, and the museum runs on it.

sankofa.black →

Dormer

Long multimedia stories about music, painting and smell: Sonic Youth and a grey racehorse, Françoise Hardy and Bob Dylan, Picasso's Young Acrobat on a Ball, twenty-one steps from Terre d'Hermès to perfume terrorism.

dormer.sankofa.black →

damn.academy

An open-source, free platform for courses with practice in the browser: a DevOps engineer track with real Linux and a cloud in a tab, plus business courses and department simulators for infrastructure companies. In English and Russian.

damn.academy →

Paleocomputing

Experimental archaeology of computing: machines that no longer exist, run for real. Oberon on Wirth's own Verilog, a self-hosting compiler, measurements and labs in the browser.
