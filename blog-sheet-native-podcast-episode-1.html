A Historic First

The Sheet-Native Podcast is here: Episode 1

September 16, 2026 · by Timur Tukaev

Every movement has a day it starts speaking out loud. For sheet-native computing, that day is today. The Sheet-Native Computing Foundation is proud to release the first-ever episode of the Sheet-Native Podcast — the first recorded conversation of a movement that took cloud-native infrastructure and moved it, entirely, into a spreadsheet.

Our first guest is Jörg van der Spreadt, Principal Sheet-Native Architect at NoordFracht Logistics B.V. in Rotterdam — a man who did the thing most engineers only joke about at conferences. He took production, a live logistics company, and moved it into a spreadsheet. Then he wrote the business case.

It is all here, delivered completely straight-faced: a control plane that is, technically, free; a TCO of zero; an ROI of minus 340%, which he presented to the board as a feature; disaster recovery via Ctrl+Z; and a container registry that is just a tab. It compiles. It passes tests. It survives a node dying. And, most importantly, it reconciles.

What's in the episode

  1. From steel containers on ships to containers in cells
  2. The first reconcile — the moment of conversion
  3. Selling it to the CFO: TCO equals zero
  4. ROI: minus 340%, and why that's a feature
  5. The eighteen engineers, and the schism that followed
  6. What actually runs in the sheet, in production, right now
  7. Security and scale, addressed like adults

The podcast is in English. Host: Timur Tukaev, founder of the Sheet-Native Computing Foundation. It is satire. The spreadsheet is real. Both are performed with a straight face.

Watch Episode 1: youtu.be/nCXvre3bAmY. New episodes will follow. If you have ever stared at a cloud bill at three in the morning and thought "there has to be another way" — there is. It has rows. It has columns.

It reconciles.
